How to install BridgeX
==================

BridgeX works on any Mac with macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel.

1. In this window, drag BridgeX onto the Applications folder.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click BridgeX.

   The first time, macOS stops it with a message like:
   "Apple could not verify 'BridgeX' is free of malware..."
   Click Done. (Don't click Move to Trash.)

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down to the Security section. Next to
   "'BridgeX' was blocked to protect your Mac", click Open Anyway.
   Confirm with Open Anyway and your password or Touch ID.

4. BridgeX opens. You only have to do this once.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older there's a shortcut instead of steps 2-3:
right-click (or Control-click) BridgeX in Applications, choose Open, then Open.


Why does macOS warn about BridgeX?
------------------------------
BridgeX isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate yet, so macOS asks
you to confirm that you trust it before it opens the first time.


If macOS says BridgeX "is damaged and can't be opened"
--------------------------------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and run this line, then open BridgeX again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BridgeX.app


Uninstalling
------------
Drag BridgeX from Applications to the Trash.
