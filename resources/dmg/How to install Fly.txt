How to install Fly
==================

Fly works on any Mac with macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel.

1. In this window, drag Fly onto the Applications folder.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Fly.

   The first time, macOS stops it with a message like:
   "Apple could not verify 'Fly' is free of malware..."
   Click Done. (Don't click Move to Trash.)

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   Scroll down to the Security section. Next to
   "'Fly' was blocked to protect your Mac", click Open Anyway.
   Confirm with Open Anyway and your password or Touch ID.

4. Fly opens. You only have to do this once.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older there's a shortcut instead of steps 2-3:
right-click (or Control-click) Fly in Applications, choose Open, then Open.


Why does macOS warn about Fly?
------------------------------
Fly isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer certificate yet, so macOS asks
you to confirm that you trust it before it opens the first time.


If macOS says Fly "is damaged and can't be opened"
--------------------------------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities) and run this line, then open Fly again:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fly.app


Uninstalling
------------
Drag Fly from Applications to the Trash.
